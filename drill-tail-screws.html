Self-Drilling Fasteners for Practical Industrial Applications

FAQ

Drill-Tail Screw Buyer Questions

What are drill-tail screws?

Drill-tail screws are self-drilling fasteners with a drill-point design that allows the screw to drill and fasten suitable materials during installation.

Are drill-tail screws also called self-drilling screws?

Yes. "Self-drilling screws" is a commonly used English product term for drill-point fasteners. Product terminology can vary by market.
